Front-panel DIM, raw status bytes
Babyface Pro (non-FS, 2015), CachyOS, kernel 7.2.4-1-cachyos, x86_64
2026-09-13

Method
------
Logged the four bytes bf_panel_tick() reads whenever they change, at the
default panel_poll_ms.  Read-only: the poll already does this read every
tick, nothing extra is written to the device.  The patch is on
davfre/babyface-pro-linux branch `staging` as "local: log raw panel
status bytes on change", behind panel_debug=1.

  sudo insmod snd-usb-babyface-pro.ko panel_debug=1
  sudo dmesg -w | grep panel

Trace
-----
DIM pressed, twice, then OUT pressed once:

  panel 00 25 8d 40 -> 00 25 8d 60      DIM down
  panel 00 25 8d 60 -> 00 25 8d 40      DIM released
  panel 00 25 8d 40 -> 00 25 8d 60      DIM down
  panel 00 25 8d 60 -> 00 25 8d 40      DIM released
  panel 00 25 8d 40 -> 00 26 8d 48      OUT down
  panel 00 26 8d 48 -> 00 26 8d 40      OUT released

st[3] flashes 0x60 for DIM and 0x48 for OUT over the 0x40 idle base.
st[1] does not move for DIM at all, across repeated presses.  The OUT
press in the same trace does move it, 0x25 -> 0x26, which is the output
selector in st[1] & 0x07 going 5 -> 6, so the byte is being read
correctly.

Controls, polled while pressing
------------------------------
  dim=on  btn=6  wheel=19     DIM pressed
  dim=on  btn=6  wheel=25     wheel turned, tracks
  dim=on  btn=4  wheel=25     OUT pressed
  dim=on  btn=6  wheel=25     DIM pressed again

Front Panel Button decodes both buttons correctly, 6 for DIM and 4 for
OUT.  Front Panel Dim reads `on` permanently and never changes.

Reading
-------
st[1] & 0x20 is a readback of the dim indicator flag.  On this unit the
flag was already set at power-on, and nothing the button does changes
it, so the control sits pinned at `on`.  The only thing that moves it is
a host write of 0x17 wVal=0x2000 wIdx=0x2000.

This differs from the FS, where the maintainer reports the press moves
Front Panel Dim and does not move Front Panel Button.  Both were
measured on real hardware, so it looks like the FS toggles the flag in
firmware and the non-FS does not.

After the driver gained a host-side toggle on the st[3] flash edge, the
button works here and Front Panel Dim tracks it off/on/off, since the
host write is now what moves the flag.
